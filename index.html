Interactive Media Assignment 1

Sculpting Harmony: Frank Gehry and the Walt Disney Concert Hall

1. What was the first thing you paid attention to when interacting with the experience?

The first things I noticed were the colours, scribbles, artistic style and the large typography. These immediately caught my attention and made the experience feel more artistic and creative than a traditional informational website.

2. Spend two minutes with the experience and create a list of each of your discrete actions.

During my two-minute interaction, I mainly scrolled, clicked, pressed buttons, moved my cursor and browsed through the experience. Scrolling was the main way I moved through the website, while clicking and pressing buttons allowed me to progress through different sections.

3. What part of the experience did you spend the most time engaging with?

I spent the most time on the opening section, particularly the large "Sculpting Harmony" text. Moving my cursor over the text caused additional words and visual elements to appear, which made me want to explore the interaction further.

4. What was the most common action in your two minute interaction with the experience?

The most common action was scrolling. It was the main way I progressed through the experience and discovered new parts of the story.

5. What is your impression of the intended primary goal of the interactive experience?

I think the main goal is to present information about the Walt Disney Concert Hall and its design process through an artistic and visual story. As I scroll, I feel like I am moving through the building and discovering different parts of its story.

6. How does the interactive experience communicate this primary goal?

The experience uses its visual style, animation, typography and storytelling to communicate its purpose. It feels connected to the architecture while presenting information in an engaging way without overwhelming the user with large amounts of text.

7. What is your impression of how the experience should be interacted with over time? (For how long and how many different times)

I think the experience is intended to be explored slowly in one sitting rather than quickly clicked through. I would expect a user to spend around 10–20 minutes going through the different sections. It feels more like a complete journey that is experienced from beginning to end than something that needs to be repeatedly visited.

8. How does the interactive experience communicate how it should be interacted with over time?

The website gradually reveals new information, visuals and interactions as the user scrolls. This encourages the user to continue moving through the experience and discover what comes next. The continuous scrolling also makes the experience feel like a journey.

9. What other media forms (digital or otherwise) does the experience reference?

The experience reminds me of digital art, documentaries, museum exhibitions and virtual tours. Its combination of archival material, visual storytelling and interactive elements makes it feel similar to an online exhibition.

10. What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should act when engaging with your research experience?

These references encourage me to slow down and explore the experience rather than quickly search for information. I feel encouraged to look closely at the visuals, follow the story and discover information as I move through the website.

11. What does this reference/s communicate to you about how you should feel when engaging with your research experience?

The artistic and exhibition-like style makes the experience feel immersive and creative. It makes me feel curious about what will appear next and more interested in discovering the story behind the building.

12. What is the most frustrating part of the interaction to you and what makes that part frustrating?

The most frustrating part was that some interactive elements were not immediately obvious. At times, I was unsure whether something was interactive or simply part of the visual design, so I had to explore the page to discover what I could interact with.

13. What is the most satisfying part of the interaction to you and what makes that part satisfying?

The most satisfying part was the opening interaction with the large “Sculpting Harmony” text. Moving my cursor over it caused additional words and visual elements to appear. I liked this because it was simple but unexpected and immediately made me curious about the rest of the experience.
